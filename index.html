Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the 2026 Olympia taking place?

Friday and Saturday Finals are at Orleans Arena (4500 W Tropicana Ave), the Olympia World Fitness Expo is at the Las Vegas Convention Center (3150 Paradise Rd), and all other events are at the Palms Casino Resort (4321 W Flamingo Rd). Event dates are September 24 to 27, 2026.

Where can I buy Finals tickets?

The only authorized seller is Olympia Productions via Ticketmaster. Do not purchase Finals tickets through third parties.

Are VIP packages still available?

VIP packages for 2026 are sold out. The only authorized VIP seller is Olympia Productions via FEVO ticketing, so avoid third-party offers.

Do children need tickets?

For the Finals, children 2 and under do not need a ticket but must remain on a parent's lap; children over 2 need a ticket. For the Expo, children 5 and under get in free.

How do I book a discounted room at the host hotel?

The Palms is the official host hotel. Book discounted rates only through the official hotel and travel page. No one will call you about housing; ignore unauthorized "Official Housing Company" solicitations.

Is there transportation between venues?

Yes. Free shuttles run on a first-come, first-served basis Friday and Saturday, every 20 minutes on two routes: the Convention Center route (7:00 AM to 5:30 PM) and the Orleans Arena route (Friday 4:00 PM to 11:30 PM, Saturday 4:30 PM to 11:30 PM).

Where should I park?

Parking is available at the Palms (free parking) and at Orleans Arena.

Where do I pick up my Expo Elite badge?

At the VIP/Expo Elite counter in the lobby of the Las Vegas Convention Center, 8 AM to 5 PM on Friday, September 25 and Saturday, September 26.

Are cameras allowed?

Cameras for personal use are allowed, but no tripods or monopods, and videoing is strictly prohibited.

Can I compete at the Amateur Olympia?

Yes. Register at the official Amateur Olympia page. IFBB Pro League pro cards are awarded to the top 3 athletes in each division.

Do you offer military discounts?

Yes. See the active-duty military tickets page for details.
